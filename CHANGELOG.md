### Added
- `jaccardSimilarity(a, b)` — Jaccard index over the set of character bigrams, a stricter,

### Added
- Levenshtein (2-row), Jaro-Winkler and Sorensen-Dice similarity.
- Ranked `search()` and `bestMatch()`; dual ESM/CJS build.
